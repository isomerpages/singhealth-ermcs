<h3>Note: Abstract submission has ended</h3>
<h4><strong>Abstract Submission Eligibility:</strong></h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Authors who submit the abstracts should be either the main author or co-author.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Abstracts should be based on Risk Management-related research / innovation
/ work / process completed between 2025 to 2026.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Should your poster be chosen as an award-winning poster, <strong>up to 2 authors will be invited to go on stage to receive the award during the poster award ceremony on 15 September 2026</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Organiser reserves the right not to accept submissions that do not comply
with the requirements. <strong>All decisions are final.</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
