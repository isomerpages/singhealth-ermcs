<p></p>
<h4><strong>Abstract Submission Guidelines:</strong></h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Abstracts must be submitted in English.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Category – Please ensure to select the most appropriate category (only
ONE).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Authors – List the authors' names, starting with the presenting author.
Please do not include degrees or professional titles (Prof, A/Prof, Dr
etc.)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Institution and Department – Please indicate Institution name and Department
name in full.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Authors may submit more than one abstract. Please submit individual abstracts
separately, only one abstract can be submitted in each form.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Title of Abstract – Should be concise and indicative of the content of
the abstract.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Abstracts shall not exceed 500 words of text. Tables, graphs, figures
or images should not be used.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Please spell acronym in full when use for the first time it appears before
using abbreviations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Please use numerals to indicate numbers, except for beginning of sentences.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Abstract (including Poster if submission is selected) Contents - Shall
be guided by the following generic ERM Process:&nbsp;
<br>1. Identify Objectives&nbsp;
<br>2. Identify potential Risk Events&nbsp;
<br>3. Evaluate current risk mitigation / control measures&nbsp;
<br>4. Prioritise, select &amp; implement additional risk mitigation measures
/ controls&nbsp;
<br>5. Monitor results and make changes as needed</p>
</li>
</ul>
